# Invitation (guest surface)

Scope: `/` and `/i/[token]`, the invitation a guest opens from WhatsApp on a phone. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: 50 to 60 guests, grandparents to close friends, Hebrew first. Job: read the invitation, answer in under a minute.
Constraints: no photographs yet (zero to two later), placeholders for names, date, venue. Secular. Two times (reception, ceremony). An invitee allowed to bring guests may add any number of named guests.
Chosen by the user after three rounds of rendered mocks, from their own references (gothic-romance printed stationery: crescent moon, thorned roses, engraved vines, blackletter). Norse: subtle.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A gilded line engraving on black, the invitation as a night garden you enter: a heart of thorns above, an iron gate below that holds your answer. It refuses the dark wedding template (centred names in a thin gold rectangle, countdown, pill button).

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground with one warm glow behind the heart. Antique-gold line work only (stems, thorns, leaves, ironwork), burgundy-filled five-petal roses with gold edges, ivory lettering, moon-white for the crescent. A braided two-strand ring and braided rules are the only Norse element. Square-cornered plates, hairline gold rules, no cards, no glow, no gradients beyond the single ground glow.

STORY: The guest sees their name, whose wedding, when and where, and that the gate is where they answer. They answer, the gate opens to a confirmation, and they can come back and change it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 390 x 844. Moon phases arc across the top. Thorn heart centred in the upper third, a crescent in a braided ring inside it. Personal greeting line, the two names stacked large in ivory, the Latin names small in blackletter gold, the date. The pointed iron gate rises from the bottom edge with a burgundy RSVP plate inside it. Vines climb the gate posts.
